The problem

0Given

U10 m/swind, left to right
a0.15 mcylinder radius
T15 °C = 288.15 Kair temperature
p∞101.3 kPaair pressure
R287.05 J/(kg·K)gas constant, dry air
γ1.4ratio of specific heats

?Find

V(x, y),  p(x, y),  p on the body,  L′, D′

The air

1Density

ρ = pR T

ρ = 101 300287.05 × 288.15 = 1.225 kg/m3

v = 1ρ = 0.817 m3/kg

2Speed of sound

c = √γ R T

c = √1.4 × 287.05 × 288.15 = 340.3 m/s

3Viscosity

μ = μ0 (TT0)3/2 T0 + ST + S

μ = 1.716×10−5 (288.15273.15)3/2 383.55398.55 = 1.79×10−5 Pa·s

Can we ignore compression and friction?

4Mach number

M = Uc

M = 10340.3 = 0.029

M < 0.3  ⇒  ρ = const  ✓

5Reynolds number

Re = ρ U Dμ

Re = 1.225 × 10 × 0.301.79×10−5 = 2.05×105

Re ≫ 1  ⇒  real air separates and leaves a wake

ideal flow drops μ:  no wake, no drag

Build the flow

6Ideal flow

∇ · V = 0,   ∇ × V = 0  ⇒  V = ∇φ,   ∇2φ = 0

linear  ⇒  solutions add

7Stream + doublet

φ = U x + κ cos θr,   κ = U a2

φ = U cos θ ( r + a2r )

8Velocity

vr = ∂φ∂r = U (1 − a2r2) cos θ

vθ = 1r ∂φ∂θ = −U (1 + a2r2) sin θ

9On the body, r = a

vr = 0   ✓ no air through the wall

vθ = −2U sin θ

|v|max = 2U = 20 m/s   at θ = ±90°

Pressure

10Bernoulli

p + ½ ρ V2 = p∞ + ½ ρ U2

Cp = p − p∞½ ρ U2 = 1 − V2U2

11On the body

Cp(θ) = 1 − 4 sin2θ

q = ½ ρ U2 = ½ × 1.225 × 102 = 61.2 Pa

front, θ = 180°:   Cp = +1  ⇒  p − p∞ = +61.2 Pa

top, θ = 90°:   Cp = −3  ⇒  p − p∞ = −183.7 Pa

12Force

L′ = −∫02π p a sin θ dθ,   D′ = −∫02π p a cos θ dθ

Cp(θ) = Cp(π − θ) = Cp(−θ)  ⇒  L′ = 0,  D′ = 0

d′Alembert’s paradox: zero drag is the price of μ = 0

Add spin

13Vortex

vθ = −U (1 + a2r2) sin θ − Γ2π r

Γ = 2π a2 ω = 2π a U S,   S = ω aU

S = 1.5  ⇒  ω = 100 rad/s = 955 rpm

Γ = 2π × 0.15 × 10 × 1.5 = 14.14 m2/s

14Where the air stops

vθ(a, θs) = 0  ⇒  sin θs = −Γ4π a U

sin θs = −14.144π × 0.15 × 10 = −0.75  ⇒  θs = −48.6°, −131.4°

S > 2  ⇒  |sin θs| > 1: stagnation point leaves the body

15Lift

L′ = ρ U Γ   (Kutta–Joukowski)

L′ = 1.225 × 10 × 14.14 = 173.1 N/m

∮ p on the surface, 20 000 panels:  L′ = 173.1 N/m,  D′ ≈ 10−15  ✓

16Fastest air

vmax = 2U + Γ2π a = 20 + 15 = 35 m/s

p − p∞ = ½ρ(U2 − vmax2) = −689 Pa

What temperature and pressure change

17Streamlines

ψ = U ( r − a2r ) sin θ + Γ2π ln ra

no ρ, no T, no p  ⇒  the picture never moves

18Forces

Δp ∝ ρ,   L′ ∝ ρ,   ρ = pR T

sea level, 15 °Cρ = 1.225L′ = 173.1 N/m
cold, −20 °Cρ = 1.394L′ = 197.1 N/m  (+13.8%)
hot, 35 °Cρ = 1.145L′ = 161.9 N/m  (−6.5%)
mile high, 83 kPa, 5 °Cρ = 1.040L′ = 147.0 N/m  (−15.1%)

The other bodies

19Half body

ψ = U r sin θ + m θ2π,   body: ψ = m2

far width 2a = mU  ⇒  m = 2 × 10 × 0.15 = 3.0 m2/s

nose:  x = −m2π U = −4.8 cm

20Oval

ψ = U y + m2π (θ1 − θ2),   xs = ± √b2 + m bπ U

xs = ±√0.0225 + 0.0225 = ±21.2 cm

Ideal flow on the screen

21Moving cylinder (home page)

W = U − Vc

V = U + a2r2 [ W − 2 (W · r̂) r̂ ]

the air sees the wind relative to the moving body

22Tracer step (RK2)

xn+½ = xn + ½ V(xn) Δt

xn+1 = xn + V(xn+½) Δt

Now switch friction on

23What ideal flow got wrong

D′ = 0  (step 12)    vs    every measurement: D′ > 0

the missing term is viscosity: air sticks to the surface

24Navier–Stokes

∇ · V = 0

∂V∂t + (V · ∇) V = −1ρ ∇p + ν ∇2V

ideal flow is this with ν = 0 and no spin in the air

25One number left

x → x/D,  V → V/U,  t → tU/D,  p → p/ρU2

∂V∂t + (V · ∇) V = −∇p + 1Re ∇2V,   Re = U Dν

same Re  ⇒  same picture, whatever the size or speed

26A case that fits

ν = 1.461×10−5 m2/s  (15 °C, sea level)

D = 3 mm,  Re = 100  ⇒  U = Re νD = 100 × 1.461×10−50.003 = 0.487 m/s

steps 0–22 used Re = 2×105: far too turbulent to resolve live

Solving it

27Grid

180 × 80 cells,  h = D/10,  domain 18 D × 8 D

staggered: p at centres, u and v on faces

28Projection step

V* = Vn + Δt [ −(V·∇)V + 1Re ∇2V ]

∇2p = 1Δt ∇ · V*

Vn+1 = V* − Δt ∇p  ⇒  ∇ · Vn+1 = 0

max |∇ · V| ≈ 10−14

29The cylinder

inside the cylinder:  V* ← 0

F = 1Δt Σinside V* h2

the push needed to stop the air there is the air’s force on the cylinder

30Time step

Δt = min( 0.4 h|u|max + |v|max ,  0.4 h2 Re4 )

Δt = min( 0.4 × 0.12.02 , 0.4 × 0.01 × 1004 ) = min(0.0198, 0.1) = 0.0198

31Edges

inletV = ideal flow past the cylinder (step 8)so the inlet does not squeeze the flow
top, bottom, outletp = 0,  ∂V/∂n = 0open

What comes out

32Coefficients

CD = Fx½ ρ U2 D,   CL = Fy½ ρ U2 D,   St = f DU

Re = 100:   CD = 1.52,   CL = ± 0.36,   St = 0.169

33Back to real units

f = St UD = 0.169 × 0.4870.003 = 27.5 Hz

D′ = CD ½ ρ U2 D = 1.52 × ½ × 1.225 × 0.4872 × 0.003 = 0.66 mN/m

27 Hz: a low hum. This is why wires sing in wind.

Result

✓

ideal:  Vbody ≤ 2U,  Δp ∈ [−3q, +q],  D′ = 0,  spin lift L′ = ρUΓ
