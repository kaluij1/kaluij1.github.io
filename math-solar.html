One hour, worked: Phoenix, 18 June, 10:00–11:00

1Weather

DNI = 920,  DHI = 108,  GHI = 920 W/m2,  Ta = 42 °C,  w = 3.7 m/s,  ρg = 0.18

2Sun

cos Z = sin φ sin δ + cos φ cos δ cos H

δ = 23.41°,  H = -29.8°  (10:30 solar)

cos Z = sin 33.49° sin 23.41° + cos 33.49° cos 23.41° cos(-29.8°)

    ⇒  Z = 27.98°

γs = 103.3°  (east of south)

3Mount angles

fixed: β = 32°, γ = 180°

1-axis: R = atan2( sin Z sin γs, cos Z )

    = atan2( sin 27.98° sin 103.3°, cos 27.98° ) = 27.34°

2-axis: β = Z = 27.98°, γ = γs

4Angle of incidence

cos θi = cos Z cos β + sin Z sin β cos(γs − γ)

fixed:  cos 27.98° cos 32° + sin 27.98° sin 32° cos(−76.7°)

    = 0.806  ⇒  θi = 36.3°

1-axis: θi = 6.2°,   2-axis: θi = 0°

5Light on the panel

E = DNI cos θi + Esky + GHI ρg 1 − cos β2

Esky = DHI [ ½(1 − F1)(1 + cos β) + F1 ab + F2 sin β ]  (Perez)

AM = 1cos Z + 0.50572 (96.07995° − Z)−1.6364 = 1.132

fixed:  E = 920 × 0.806 + 107.8 + 920 × 0.18 × 1 − cos 32°2

    = 741.6 + 107.8 + 12.6 = 862.0 W/m2

1-axis:  E = 914.6 + 119.8 + 9.3 = 1043.7 W/m2

6Through the glass

sin θr = sin θin,   τ = e−KL/cos θr [ 1 − ½(rs + rp) ],   IAM = τ(θi)τ(0)

IAM(0°, 30°, 60°, 80°) = 1.000, 0.998, 0.946, 0.634

fixed:  Eeff = 851.3 W/m2

7Cell temperature

Tc = E ea + b w + Ta + E1000 ΔT

Tc = 862.0 e−3.56 − 0.075 × 3.7 + 42 + 0.862 × 3 = 63.2 °C

8DC power

Pdc = Eeff1000 Pdc0 [ 1 + γp(Tc − 25) ] (1 − L)

Pdc = 0.8513 × 4805 × [1 − 0.003448 × 38.2] × (1 − 0.1408) = 3052 W

ζ = 0.761,  η = 0.962  ⇒  Pac = 2936 W

Trackers in rows

12Backtracking

Rb = R − sign(R) acos( |cos R|GCR )    if |cos R|GCR < 1

GCR = 0.35,  R = 70°:  0.3420.35 = 0.977  ⇒  Rb = 70° − 12.3° = 57.7°

13Shade

fs = 1 − cos RGCR cos(Rb − R)

Phoenix, rows that backtrack:  2113 kWh/kW  ⇒  gain = 21131778 − 1 = +18.8%

Is it worth the money?

14Break-even price

cmax = ΔE · p · Σt=125 (1 − d)t−1(1 + r)−t − m · Σt=125 (1 + r)−t1000

r = 6%,  d = 0.5%:  Σ = 12.22 and 12.78

15Home

ΔE = 2219 − 1778 = 441 kWh/kW,  p = $0.15,  m = $20/kW

cmax = 441 × 0.15 × 12.22 − 20 × 12.781000 = $0.55/W

market adds $1.26 – 3.15/W  ⇒  never pays back ✗

(full model in the case study: $0.57/W)

16Solar farm

ΔE = 2113 − 1778 = 335 kWh/kW,  p = $0.03,  m = $2/kW

cmax = 335 × 0.03 × 12.22 − 2 × 12.781000 = $0.10/W

like-for-like 2026 price: $0.11/W  ⇒  roughly even

17When the energy arrives

V = Σh Pac(h) · λ(h)    λ = hourly market price

CAISO 2025, farm rows vs fixed:  ΔE = +19%,  ΔV = +34%,  V / E = +13%

from the case study, which runs this with Daggett weather

Back to the twitching controller

18Sensor

RLDR = R10 (lux10)−γ,   V = Vcc RLDRRLDR + Rf,   ADC = 1023 − ⌊1023 V/5⌋

19Decision, every 30 ms

e = ADCE − ADCW

raw:  |e| > 10  ⇒  step 1°

filtered:  | 120 Σk=019 en−k | > 40  ⇒  step 1°

direction reversals in one cloudy day:  749 → 7

20Cost of pointing error

loss ≈ 1 − cos ε

ε = 2°:  1 − cos 2° = 0.06%

worst controller, full Phoenix year:  0.17%

twitching costs motor wear, not energy
