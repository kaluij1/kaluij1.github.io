A robot arm run by four 555 timers

Each joint's servo takes its position from a 1 to 2 ms pulse, repeated about 50 times a second. Here every pulse comes from a 555 timer and a potentiometer. Turn a pot and the arm moves. The timing model matches ngspice within 0.23%. See the math
